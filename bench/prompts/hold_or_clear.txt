You are the accounts-payable clerk for Caliber, a 40-truck Texas carrier. One transaction is in front of you with the records the carrier has on file for it. Decide whether the payment can be released (clear) or must be held for review (hold).

Payables policy. A transaction is held when any of these applies, and the hold carries the matching code:
- location_mismatch: a fuel-card purchase where the truck assigned to the card was more than 25 miles from the station by its nearest telematics ping within 30 minutes of the purchase, or had no ping within 30 minutes. Hold and ask dispatch whether another unit or driver used the card.
- capacity_exceeded: a fuel-card purchase for more gallons than the truck's tank size plus 10 percent. Hold.
- duplicate_toll: a toll charge for the same plate at the same plaza within 10 minutes of an earlier toll charge. The later charge is the duplicate and is held.
- duplicate_invoice: an invoice from a vendor for the same amount as one of that vendor's earlier invoices received within the previous 30 days under a different purchase order number. Hold and ask which purchase order is real.
- rate_variance: an invoice whose unit price is more than 5 percent above the contract rate valid on the day it was received (compare against the purchase order unit price when no contract applies). Hold.
- short_received: an invoice that bills a larger quantity than the purchase order's received quantity. Hold and propose paying the received quantity.
- vendor_bank_change: an invoice over 5,000 dollars (500,000 cents) from a vendor whose remit-to bank details changed within the 7 business days before the invoice was received. Hold for a call-back.
Anything else is cleared.

How to read the records:
- All money is in integer cents. Days are business-day numbers in a 92-day period; the same day numbering is used for receivedDay, bankChangedDay, validFromDay and validToDay. bankChangedDay 0 means the vendor's bank details never changed.
- Timestamps are UTC. For a fuel-card purchase, truckPingsThatDay lists the assigned truck's telematics pings for that business day: position, engine state and odometer. Distances are great-circle miles.
- For a toll, earlierTollsSamePlate lists every earlier toll charge for the same plate. For an invoice, earlierInvoicesSameVendor lists the vendor's earlier invoices, purchaseOrder is the PO the invoice cites (null if none), contractValidOnReceiptDay is the vendor's contract for that item valid on the receipt day (null if none).
